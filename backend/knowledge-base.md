## 1. Business Overview

<!-- roles: admin, staff, customer -->

Glow By Miral is a premium beauty parlor, salon, and spa offering professional hair, skin, nail, and wellness services. We are committed to delivering a luxury experience with highly trained staff and curated beauty products.

The platform operates on three user roles:

- **Admin** — Manages the full business: staff, clients, services, appointments, products, reports, and settings.
- **Staff** — Provides services to clients; manages their own appointments, consultations, and treatment plans.
- **Customer** — Books appointments, browses and orders products, and accesses their personal records.

New customers and staff accounts are created by the admin. There is no self-registration — this ensures quality control and a curated client experience.

## 2. Appointments

<!-- roles: admin, staff, customer -->

### Booking an Appointment

Customers can book appointments through the Appointments section of their dashboard by clicking "Book Appointment". When booking, you select:

- The service you want
- Your preferred staff member
- A date and time

Once booked, the appointment is assigned to the selected staff member, and both the client and the relevant staff are notified.

### Appointment Statuses

An appointment moves through the following statuses:

- **Pending** — Awaiting confirmation by staff or admin.
- **Confirmed** — Appointment is confirmed and scheduled.
- **In Progress** — The service is currently being delivered.
- **Completed** — The service has been delivered successfully.
- **Cancelled** — The appointment was cancelled.
- **No Show** — The client did not attend without notice.

### Viewing Appointments

Customers can view all their upcoming and past appointments from the Appointments page. The view supports both a list layout and a calendar layout.

---

## 3. Booking Policy

<!-- roles: admin, staff, customer -->

- Appointments must be booked in advance through the dashboard.
- Walk-ins are subject to availability and must be confirmed by a staff member or admin.
- Each appointment is linked to one service and one staff member.
- Customers may book multiple appointments, but each must be scheduled separately.
- Appointment times are based on the service duration. Arriving late may result in a shortened session or rescheduling.

---

## 4. Cancellation Policy

<!-- roles: admin, staff, customer -->

- Appointments can be cancelled at no charge up to **24 hours before** the scheduled start time.
- Cancellations made **within 24 hours** of the appointment may incur no cancellation fee.
- To cancel, open the appointment in your dashboard and select "Cancel".
- Repeated last-minute cancellations or no-shows may affect your ability to book future appointments.

---

## 5. Refund Policy

<!-- roles: admin, staff, customer -->

- Services that have already been rendered are **non-refundable**.
- If a service was incomplete due to a fault on our end, a partial or full refund may be issued at the discretion of the admin.
- Product purchases may be returned within **7 days** of delivery, provided the product is **unused, unopened, and in original packaging**.
- Opened or used products are not eligible for return unless they are defective.
- Refund requests for products should be raised through the in-app chat with our team.

## 6. Services

<!-- roles: admin, staff, customer -->

### Service Categories

Services at Glow By Miral are grouped into categories such as:

- Hair (cuts, colour, treatments, styling)
- Skin (facials, peels, brightening, anti-ageing)
- Nails (manicure, pedicure, nail art, gel)
- Wellness (massage, body scrubs, aromatherapy)

### Service Variants

Many services offer variants — for example, a haircut may have a standard and a long-hair variant, with different pricing and duration. Variants are shown when booking or browsing services.

### Pricing

Each service has a base price. Variants may add a price modifier (positive or negative). Final pricing is displayed at the time of booking.

### Duration

Service durations are shown alongside each service. Please arrive on time to ensure your full session.

## 7. Products

<!-- roles: admin, staff, customer -->

### Browsing Products

Customers can browse available beauty products from the Products page. Products are organised by category and include name, price, description, and stock availability.

### Adding to Cart

Products can be added to a cart. The cart is accessible from the top navigation bar. Customers can adjust quantities or remove items before checkout.

### Placing an Order

Checkout is completed from the cart. Orders are confirmed immediately. You will receive a notification once your order is placed.

### Order Statuses

- **Pending** — Order received, awaiting processing.
- **Processing** — Order is being prepared.
- **Shipped** — Order has been dispatched.
- **Delivered** — Order has been received by the customer.
- **Cancelled** — Order was cancelled before delivery.
- **Refunded** — A refund has been issued for the order.

### Viewing Orders

All past and current orders can be viewed from the Products section under "My Orders".

### Stock

Products are only available while in stock. Out-of-stock products cannot be added to the cart.

### Staff & Products

Staff members can browse products for reference but cannot place orders.

Customers can view their own consultation records from the Consultation section of the dashboard. Staff can create records for clients they work with.

## 9. Treatment Plans

<!-- roles: admin, staff, customer -->

### What is a Treatment Plan?

A treatment plan is a structured series of sessions designed to achieve a specific beauty or wellness goal. For example: a 6-week skin brightening programme, or a monthly deep conditioning hair plan.

### How It Works

1. A staff member or admin creates a treatment plan template.
2. The plan is assigned to a specific client.
3. The client can track their progress from the Treatment Plans section.
4. Each session is logged as it is completed.

### Plan Statuses

- **Draft** — Plan is being prepared, not yet active.
- **Active** — Plan is assigned and in progress.
- **Completed** — All sessions in the plan are done.
- **Cancelled** — Plan was discontinued.

## 17. Frequently Asked Questions

<!-- roles: admin, staff, customer -->

**Q: How do I book an appointment?**
A: Go to the Appointments section in your dashboard and click "Book Appointment". Choose your service, preferred staff, and a date and time.

**Q: Can I choose which staff member serves me?**
A: Yes. When booking, you can select your preferred staff member from the available options.

**Q: How do I cancel an appointment?**
A: Open the appointment from your dashboard and click Cancel. Remember: cancellations within 24 hours may incur a fee.

**Q: How do I check my order status?**
A: Go to Products → My Orders in your dashboard to view all orders and their current status.

**Q: Can I return a product?**
A: Yes, within 7 days of delivery if the product is unused and in its original packaging. Please contact us via in-app chat to initiate a return.

**Q: How do I view my consultation records?**
A: Go to the Consultation section of your dashboard to see all records your staff has created for you.

**Q: What is a treatment plan?**
A: A treatment plan is a personalised programme of sessions designed to help you achieve a specific beauty or wellness goal. Your staff will create and assign it to you.

**Q: How do I contact the salon?**
A: Use the in-app chat to message our team directly. Staff and admins are available during business hours.

**Q: Can I update my profile details?**
A: Yes. Go to Profile in your dashboard and update your name, phone number, or avatar.

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
A: On the login page, click "Forgot Password" and follow the instructions sent to your registered email.

**Q: Why can't I see the Products cart option?**
A: The cart is only available to customers. Staff members can browse products but cannot place orders.

**Q: How are notifications delivered?**
A: Notifications appear instantly in the bell icon in the top navigation bar. You will be notified for appointments, orders, messages, and treatment plan updates.
